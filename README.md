# 100fx-phone-handoff

A FreeX accessibility proposal for continuing a ChatGPT conversation through an opt-in FaceTime call on an iPhone or Mac.

## Proposed experience

1. Ask the assistant to call a device you choose.
2. Preview the AI caller’s name and avatar, then approve the call.
3. Answer or decline. During the conversation, pause the assistant and take over privately for sign-in.
4. End the call at any time.

Offer fictional personas or a real person’s likeness with their permission. Identify the caller as AI throughout.

## Project status

Design proposal only. The first feasibility test is whether a supported Apple interface lets an AI participant originate a FaceTime call and supply live audio and video. No calling service or ChatGPT integration has been deployed.
